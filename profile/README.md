## About

**mirangod** is an independent game development initiative
focused on building original games and interactive experiences.

Our goal is to turn simple ideas into memorable gameplay,
with a focus on creativity, experimentation and accessibility.

## Projects

### 🧀 Pegue o Queijo
A 2D platformer where a little rat must collect cheese
and return safely to its home.

*Status: In development*

### 👺 Fuja do Goblin
A logic game where players must solve challenges while
avoiding a fast-moving goblin.

*Status: Playable prototype*
